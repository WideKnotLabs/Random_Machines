Arp Magic

Keys, plucks, bells, winds and voices, each sound playing an arpeggio idiom, from a Bach prelude to a gamelan interlock. Most are recorded instruments: pianos, mallets, harps, guitars, guzheng, flutes and choir.

Download 60.8 MB · Free while the INK Synth beta is open

In INK Synth, tap Import in the Library and choose the file, or open it in INK from Files or AirDrop. Save and share sounds
