Arp Magic

Keys, plucks, bells, winds and voices, each sound playing an arpeggio idiom, from a Bach prelude to a gamelan interlock. Most are recorded instruments: pianos, mallets, harps, guitars, guzheng, flutes and choir.

Download 60.8 MB · Free while the INK Synth beta is open

In INK Synth, tap Import in the Library and choose the file, or open it in INK from Files or AirDrop. Save and share sounds

  1. Handpan Cycle

    A hang in D minor as a handpan groove: the ding, then the tone field, call and answer.

  2. Ocarina Song

    A breathy ocarina in a slow song figure: legato for three bars, detached in the fourth.

  3. Romanza Guitar

    INK's nylon guitar on Giuliani's right-hand formulas: p-a-m-i, answered by p-i-m-a.

  4. Orinoco Pizz

    Cello pizzicato as an Enya ostinato, eighth-note echoes across a big hall.

  5. Whisper Lyre

    A ten-string lyre in harp bisbigliando: the chord whispered between the hands at 32nds.

  6. Bright Gliss

    Concert harp glissandi: eight notes swept up the chord four times a bar, every string ringing.

  7. Osiris Nocturne

    An upright piano nocturne: root and fifth below, the top ringing over a moving inner voice.

  8. Pedal Waterfall

    A Steinway grand in the waterfall: the bass, then eight notes falling from the top, under the pedal.

  9. Tenth Lullaby

    A soft upright in the Yiruma tenth: bass, fifth, tenth and back, one wave a bar, pedalled.

  10. Gate Pluck

    A supersaw trance arp: the chord in 16ths, an octave higher each beat, four octaves a bar.

  11. Pixel Run

    A square chip voice flicking five notes up and back at 32nds.

  12. Horn Call

    A French horn calling the triad up and back, slurred, then tongued.

  13. Tine Bloom

    A tine electric piano in a ballad spread: bass and top, then out from the middle, all held.

  14. Khim Tremolo

    A Thai khim's tremolo on the tune's note while the thumb climbs the chord beneath.

  15. Starfall Glock

    Orchestra bells under soft mallets: one Lydian cascade a bar, as in a film score.

  16. Neo-Soul Rhodes

    A Mark I Rhodes ballad: the bass low, rolled chord tones wandering over it.

  17. Kumbengo

    A bright harp in the kora's kumbengo: thumbs on the bass, fingers above.

  18. Rootless Vibes

    Vibraphone in Bill Evans voicings, open and damped by turns.

  19. Prelude Marimba

    A full marimba on the figure of Bach's Prelude in C, echoes bouncing left and right.

  20. Glass Adagio

    Tuned glasses after Mozart's Adagio for glass harmonica: one slow arch a bar, left ringing.

  21. Dayvan Tape

    A warm synth on worn tape in a Boards of Canada arp, with long echoes.

  22. Toccata Clav

    A clavinet toccata: the bass struck between every note of a rising line.

  23. Rokudan Shirabe

    A taishogoto in koto danmono figures: the top ringing, the inner voice walking.

  24. Drumming Wood

    Soft-mallet marimba in a Reich build-up: the rests of the grid fill in, bar by bar.

  25. Delay Spires

    A clean Gretsch electric: straight 8ths into a dotted-eighth echo.

  26. Fishing Boat Glissandi

    Guzheng cascades from Fishing Boat Song at Dusk, falling, then rising.

  27. Choir Entries

    An SATB choir entering voice by voice from the bass up: four, then eight.

  28. Additive Organ

    A Renaissance chamber organ in a toccata: a pedal point under a rising, then falling hand.

  29. Sikuri Hocket

    Two Andean pan flutes trading notes in hocket.

  30. Kotekan

    Balinese gamelan bronzes in kotekan interlock.

  31. Sugar Plum Cascade

    Celesta cascades after the Sugar Plum Fairy, doubling in speed into each cadence.

  32. Silver Flute

    One flute in Ravel's daybreak murmurs, thinning and returning.

  33. Harp Arabesque

    Pedal harp in Debussy's first Arabesque: groups of three over the 16ths.

  34. Fox Night

    Felt and glass cells after Elijah Fox, growing from six notes to eight.
