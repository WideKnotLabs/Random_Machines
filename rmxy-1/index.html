RMXY-1 · For iPhone and iPad

The most powerful FX chain, everywhere you go.

App StoreComing soon

One move. Every effect.

Each parameter has a Base value and a target on X and Y. Drag across the pad and the whole chain moves with you.

8 slots.

Load any effect into any slot, in any order.

19 effects.

Plus an LFO, a MIDI lane or another AUv3 in any slot.

Endless possibilities.

Roll the dice and the whole chain is new: effects, order and every mapping.

Built to perform.

Everything you reach for on stage is on the main screen.

  1. HoldLift your finger. The pad stays put.
  2. Motion recordOne bar of pad movement, repeating in time.
  3. DecayHow the chain glides back, 0 to 5 seconds.
  4. DiceA whole new chain in one tap.
  5. BrakeMute, vinyl stop, reverse or glitch.

Record a loop.
Keep playing.

Capture one, two or four bars, synced to tempo or free. Overdub it, recapture it, and run it back through the chain while you work the pad.

  • 1, 2 or 4 barsSynced or free
  • OverdubLayer as it plays
  • RecaptureGrab what you just heard
  • Bypass DryOnly the loop goes through

Make sound.
Or shape it.

No input needed.

Three of the nineteen make sound on their own. Roll the dice, then map what you find to the pad.

  • NoiseWhite, pink, green or blue.
  • OSCMulti saw, sync, X-mod or PWM.
  • ShepardA tone that rises or falls forever.

Drop it into your set.

Run it on its own with your audio interface, or load it on every channel of your host.

Plug in your whole studio.

RMXY-1 works with class-compliant USB audio and MIDI gear. Run a drum machine or a synth through the chain, and send MIDI back out to it.

Six MIDI lanes send CC or notes and follow the pad. One move changes your effects and your hardware together.

  • USB audioIn and out
  • 6 MIDI lanesCC or notes
  • MIDI clockSync to your gear
  • AUv3MIDI through the host
