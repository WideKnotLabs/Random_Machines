RMXY-1

A live audio performance effect surface for iPhone and iPad.

RMXY-1 processes audio locally on device and does not upload, store, or share audio on our servers. The standalone app does not request microphone access. The AUv3 extension processes audio provided by the host app.

Mode Standalone and AUv3
Processing Real-time local audio
Privacy No account, analytics, or cloud upload

Features

  • Real-time audio effect processing
  • Standalone mode without microphone permission
  • AUv3 mode for compatible iOS host apps
  • Designed for full-screen touch performance
  • Audio processing happens locally on the device
  • No account required
  • No third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or cloud audio upload

Compatibility

RMXY-1 is available for iPhone and iPad. AUv3 mode requires a compatible iOS audio host.

The standalone app does not currently provide background audio. When RMXY-1 is used as an AUv3 extension, audio behavior is controlled by the host app.

Privacy

RMXY-1 does not send audio, MIDI, analytics, identifiers, or user data off the device.

RMXY-1 does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
