RMXY-1 Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026

RMXY-1 processes audio locally on your device and does not collect app data from users.

Published by Borja Deudero Gracia under the Random Machines name.

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how RMXY-1 handles information when you use the app on iPhone or iPad.

Summary

RMXY-1 does not collect user data from the app. Audio and MIDI processing happens locally on your device. RMXY-1 does not send audio, MIDI, analytics, identifiers, or user data to Random Machines or to any external server.

Data Collection

RMXY-1 does not collect personal data, audio recordings, MIDI data, device identifiers, analytics, crash reports, location data, contacts, photos, or account information from the app.

Audio Processing

RMXY-1 processes audio locally on device. Audio is not uploaded, stored on Random Machines servers, or shared. The standalone app does not request microphone access. The AUv3 extension processes audio provided by the host app.

AUv3 Mode

When RMXY-1 is used as an AUv3 audio unit inside a host app, RMXY-1 processes audio and MIDI provided by the host app. The host app controls audio routing, recording, background behavior, and session handling. Please review the host app's privacy policy for its own practices.

The standalone app does not currently provide background audio. When RMXY-1 is used as an AUv3 extension, audio behavior is controlled by the host app.

Local Storage

RMXY-1 may store app settings, presets, preferences, and related app data locally on your device. This information remains on your device and is not sent to Random Machines.

Retention

RMXY-1 does not retain app data on Random Machines servers. Support emails are retained only as long as needed to handle the request and maintain support records.

Tracking and Advertising

RMXY-1 does not track users. RMXY-1 does not use advertising identifiers, third-party advertising, tracking pixels, or analytics SDKs.

RMXY-1 does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Data Sharing

RMXY-1 does not sell, rent, share, or transmit user data.

Support Emails

If you contact support by email, you choose to provide your email address and message. That information is used only to respond to your support request.

Support emails are handled through our email provider and are used only to respond to support or privacy requests. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

RMXY-1 is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your Choices

You can remove locally stored app data by deleting RMXY-1 from your device.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if RMXY-1's features or data practices change. The updated policy will be posted on this page.
