Common Issues

Standalone mode

The standalone app does not request microphone access. To process host audio, use RMXY-1 as an AUv3 extension inside a compatible iOS audio host.

No sound output

Check your device volume, audio route, connected headphones or audio interface, and whether the effect is bypassed.

AUv3 host issues

When RMXY-1 is loaded inside another app, the host app controls audio routing, recording, background behavior, and session handling. Check the host app's track, input monitoring, and plugin settings.

Background audio

The standalone app does not currently provide background audio. When RMXY-1 is used as an AUv3 extension, audio behavior is controlled by the host app.

Privacy

RMXY-1 processes audio locally on device and does not upload, store, or share audio on our servers. The standalone app does not request microphone access. The AUv3 extension processes audio provided by the host app.

RMXY-1 does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
