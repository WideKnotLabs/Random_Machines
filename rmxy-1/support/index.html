Common Issues

No microphone input in standalone mode

Check that microphone access is enabled:

Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone > RMXY-1

No sound output

Check your device volume, audio route, connected headphones or audio interface, and whether the effect is bypassed.

AUv3 host issues

When RMXY-1 is loaded inside another app, the host app controls audio routing, recording, background behavior, and session handling. Check the host app's track, input monitoring, and plugin settings.

Background audio

The standalone RMXY-1 app does not use background audio. When RMXY-1 is used as an AUv3 inside a host app, background behavior is controlled by the host.

Privacy

RMXY-1 processes audio locally on your device. It does not upload audio, MIDI, analytics, identifiers, or user data.
